Registered Office Address in Singapore: Rules, Home Addresses, and Virtual Offices (2026)

📅 Updated 15 Nov 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ Karman Editorial Team

Quick answer

Every Singapore company must have a physical registered office address in Singapore from incorporation, where statutory notices can be served. Since 20 October 2026 there is no minimum public opening-hours rule, but company records must be available for inspection on reasonable notice for at least two hours each business day. P.O. boxes are not allowed. Options include a leased office, a co-working space, an eligible home address, or a registered address service.

Key takeaways

  • Official letters from ACRA, IRAS and the courts go to the registered office.
  • HDB and private home addresses can be used subject to the property rules.
  • Foreign founders incorporating remotely usually use a registered address service.
  • Lodge any change of address with ACRA within 14 days.

Every Singapore company must have a registered office address in Singapore from the day it is incorporated. It is where official letters from ACRA, IRAS, and the courts are sent, and where the company's statutory registers may be inspected. Choosing the right address is simple, but getting it wrong can lead to missed notices and penalties.

ACRA's Requirements

  • A physical address in Singapore. P.O. boxes are not allowed.
  • Records available for inspection on reasonable notice, for at least two hours on each business day. You now set your own opening hours.
  • Where registers are kept or made available, including the register of members, directors, and registrable controllers, unless kept electronically as permitted.
  • Displayed on official documents. The company's name and registration number must appear on its letters, invoices, and other official documents.
  • Kept up to date. Any change must be lodged with ACRA within 14 days.
What changed on 20 October 2026

Under the Corporate and Accounting Laws (Amendment) Act 2025, ACRA removed the old rule that a registered office must be open to the public for at least three hours every business day. Companies now set their own hours, as long as company records can be made available for inspection on reasonable notice for at least two hours on each business day. The 14-day deadline to tell ACRA about an address change is unchanged. See our full guide to the October 2026 ACRA changes.

Using Your Home Address

Singapore Citizens and PRs often use their home as the registered office for a small business. The rules depend on the property type:

HDB flats

HDB allows certain small-scale businesses to be run from flats under its home-based business schemes. The business should not cause nuisance, employ outside workers on site, or receive customers in ways that disturb neighbours. Registration with HDB is usually required before using the flat as a registered office.

Private residential property

URA's Home Office Scheme allows owners and tenants of private homes to use part of the home as a small office, subject to conditions on the type of business, the number of people working there, and no signage or customer visits. Tenants usually also need the landlord's consent.

Privacy consideration

A registered office address is public. Anyone can see it on the company's business profile. If you do not want your home address on the public record, use a registered address service or an office address instead.

Virtual Offices and Registered Address Services

Foreign founders who incorporate remotely need a Singapore address from day one. A registered address service provides a compliant address, receives and scans official mail, and keeps the registers available. When choosing one:

  • Confirm mail is scanned and sent to you quickly, since some ACRA and IRAS notices have short response times.
  • Check whether the provider is a registered corporate service provider and can also act as your corporate secretary.
  • Understand that banks and some regulators may ask where the business actually operates, so a registered address is not a substitute for real substance if you need it for tax residency or licensing.

Registered Office vs Operating Address

The registered office is where official correspondence goes. Your company can operate from a different place, such as a co-working space, a warehouse, or overseas, as long as the registered office meets ACRA's requirements. For tax residency and treaty purposes, what matters is where the company is managed and controlled, not the registered address alone.

Changing Your Registered Office

  1. Directors approve the new address by resolution.
  2. Lodge the change with ACRA through BizFile+ within 14 days.
  3. Update invoices, letterheads, your website, bank records, and IRAS and GST records where needed.
  4. Move or make available the statutory registers at the new address.

Conclusion

A registered office is a small decision with real consequences. Pick an address where official mail will reliably reach you, that you are comfortable having on the public record, and that meets ACRA's accessibility rules.
