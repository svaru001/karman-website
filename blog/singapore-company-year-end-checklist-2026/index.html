Singapore Company Year-End Checklist 2026: 10 Deadlines and Rule Changes Before 2027

📅 Published 15 Nov 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ Karman Editorial Team

Quick answer

Before the end of 2026, a Singapore company should file its YA 2026 corporate tax return (Form C-S or Form C) by 30 November 2026, appeal by 30 November if it missed the S$2,000 CIT Rebate Cash Grant, and plan for the higher Employment Pass salary floor of S$6,000 that applies to new applications from 1 January 2027. Annual returns now also require confirming officer and member details under ACRA rules in force since 20 October 2026.

Key takeaways

  • 30 Nov 2026: YA 2026 Form C-S / C-S (Lite) / Form C due, and the last day to appeal for a missing CIT Rebate Cash Grant.
  • 1 Jan 2027: EP minimum salary rises to S$6,000 (S$6,600 financial services) and S Pass to S$3,600 for new applications.
  • Companies with a 31 December year end: ECI due by 31 March 2027, AGM by 30 June 2027, annual return by 31 July 2027.
  • Employers: 2026 IR8A salary records due by 1 March 2027.

The last six weeks of 2026 are busier than usual for Singapore companies. A corporate tax deadline, a cash grant appeal window, a new set of ACRA annual return rules, and higher work pass salaries from 1 January 2027 all land at the same time. Here is everything in date order, with what to do about each.

1. File your YA 2026 corporate tax return by 30 November

Form C-S, Form C-S (Lite) and Form C for Year of Assessment 2026 (covering the financial year that ended in 2025) are due on 30 November 2026. Late filing can lead to an estimated assessment, a composition fine and, eventually, a summons. If your books are not ready, talk to your accountant now - see our guide to late filing penalties.

2. Check you received your CIT rebate and cash grant

For YA 2026, IRAS enhanced the Corporate Income Tax rebate in April 2026 to 50% of tax payable, with a total benefit (rebate plus cash grant) capped at S$40,000. Companies that made CPF contributions for at least one local employee (excluding shareholder-directors) in 2025 also qualify for a S$2,000 CIT Rebate Cash Grant, paid automatically. If you qualified but did not receive the grant, IRAS asks companies to email myTax Mail with the subject "Appeal for CIT Rebate Cash Grant" by 30 November 2026. Full details in our Budget 2026 tax guide.

3. Clean up your ACRA records before the next annual return

Since 20 October 2026, filing an annual return also confirms that ACRA's records of your directors, CEO, secretary, auditors and members are accurate, and asks who performs the CEO role. Correct any outdated addresses, ID numbers, share transfers or resigned officers in Bizfile first. Our ACRA October 2026 changes guide has a 15-minute checklist.

4. Plan hires around the 1 January 2027 work pass changes

For renewals, the higher EP salary floor applies to passes expiring from 1 January 2028, and the new COMPASS salary benchmarks to passes expiring from 1 July 2027. If you are hiring a foreign professional whose package sits between the old and new floor, budget for the new floor at renewal anyway. Founders on their own company's EP should read our COMPASS 2027 guide and run the free EP COMPASS calculator.

5. Check whether you crossed the GST threshold in 2026

If your taxable turnover for calendar year 2026 exceeded S$1 million, you must apply for GST registration within 30 days of the year end - by 30 January 2027. Use our GST registration checker or read when to register for GST.

6. Prepare 2026 employee income records

Employers must submit employees' 2026 income (IR8A and related forms) to IRAS by 1 March 2027. Companies with five or more employees, or those that have been told to, must use the Auto-Inclusion Scheme. Our payroll guide covers the details.

7. Close your December books cleanly

For companies with a 31 December financial year end, the next cycle starts on 1 January: Estimated Chargeable Income (ECI) within three months (31 March 2027, unless exempt), an AGM within six months (30 June 2027, unless dispensed with or the company qualifies for an exemption), and the annual return within seven months (31 July 2027). Reconcile bank accounts, chase unpaid invoices and record director loans before year end - it makes every one of these filings easier.

8. Decide on founder pay and dividends for 2027

Year end is the natural time to set next year's salary and dividend split. If you hold an EP, your salary has to keep pace with the 2027 floor and COMPASS benchmarks; if you do not, a lower salary and higher dividend may be more tax-efficient. Model it in our free founder pay optimizer.

9. Review dormant or unused companies

ACRA's faster striking off process means an unused company with overdue filings can be struck off more quickly than before. Either bring it up to date or close it properly.

10. Watch Budget 2027

Singapore's Budget is usually delivered in February. Founders should watch for whether a corporate tax rebate is announced for YA 2027, any changes to the Enterprise Innovation Scheme, and any further work pass adjustments. We will update our guides as soon as it is announced.

Frequently Asked Questions

The YA 2026 Form C-S, Form C-S (Lite) and Form C are due on 30 November 2026. YA 2026 covers the financial year that ended in 2025.

For YA 2026 the CIT rebate is 50% of tax payable, enhanced by IRAS in April 2026 from the 40% first announced in Budget 2026. The total benefit, including the S$2,000 cash grant for companies with a local employee in 2025, is capped at S$40,000.

For new applications from 1 January 2027 the EP minimum qualifying salary is S$6,000 a month for most sectors (up to S$11,500 for applicants aged 45 and above) and S$6,600 in financial services (up to S$12,700). The new floor applies to renewals of passes expiring from 1 January 2028.

The S Pass minimum qualifying salary rises from S$3,300 to S$3,600 for new applications from 1 January 2027, with higher amounts for older applicants and the financial services sector.

If your company made CPF contributions for at least one local employee (not counting shareholder-directors) in 2025 and is active, but did not receive the grant, IRAS asks you to email myTax Mail with the subject "Appeal for CIT Rebate Cash Grant" by 30 November 2026.
